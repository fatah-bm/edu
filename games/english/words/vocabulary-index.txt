PROMPT AI UNTUK MEMBUAT SPRITESHEET (5 gambar di atas)
========================================
Kelima topik di atas BELUM punya file gambar (assets/adjective.png, color.png,
animal.png, public-facilities.png, my-house.png) — index.html sudah mereferensikan
path tsb tapi akan tampil kosong sampai file dibuat & ditaruh di assets/.

Gaya rujukan: assets/my-body.png (lihat gambar itu untuk contoh gaya — kalau tool AI
mendukung reference image, lampirkan file itu). Kalau tidak, deskripsi gayanya:
ilustrasi flat/vector ala buku anak, garis tebal bersih, cel-shading lembut, warna
cerah ceria, bentuk membulat ramah, satu subjek per sel dengan padding merata,
TANPA teks/huruf/angka/watermark, TIDAK fotorealistis. Tiap sel punya background
pastel solid (bergantian biru/ungu/hijau/oranye pastel lembut per baris, seperti
di my-body.png).

--- Prompt 1: Adjective (assets/adjective.png) ---
Create a single flat sprite-sheet image, 5 columns x 3 rows (15 cells total,
image 640x384px, each cell exactly 128x128px). Style: flat vector children's-book
illustration matching a friendly rounded thick-outlined cartoon look (soft
cel-shading, bright cheerful colors, centered single subject per cell, even
padding), no text/letters/numbers/watermarks anywhere, not photorealistic. Each
cell has a solid flat pastel background (rotate soft pastel blue/purple/green
per row). Fill cells left-to-right, top-to-bottom, in this exact order, one
simple visual concept per cell illustrating the adjective (no text/labels):
1. big — a very large elephant filling most of the cell
2. small — a tiny ant/mouse
3. tall — a tall giraffe stretching upward
4. short — a small short potted plant/child
5. long — a long snake stretched out
6. large — a big balloon/whale shape
7. thin — a thin pencil or thin stick figure
8. fat — a round chubby hippo
9. slow — a turtle/snail
10. high — a mountain peak or a kite flying high
11. low — a small arrow pointing down / a worm low on the ground
12. pretty — a pretty flower
13. cute — a cute kitten/puppy face
14. handsome — a smiling well-dressed boy/prince character
Leave the 15th (last) cell empty/blank (same pastel background, no subject).

watermarks anywhere, not photorealistic. Each cell shows one simple rounded
paint-blob / paint-swatch shape filled with a solid color, centered with even
padding, on a plain neutral light-gray/white cell background (so the color itself
